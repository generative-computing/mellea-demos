Granite Vision 4.1 4B is a vision-language model (VLM) that delivers frontier-level performance on structured document extraction tasks—chart extraction, table extraction, and semantic key-value pair extraction—within a compact 4B parameter footprint. It offers a lightweight alternative to much larger frontier models, enabling cheap, scalable local document processing and business automation.

Two main components drive this performance. The first is a feature injection scheme inspired by DeepStack that distributes visual information across multiple LLM layers, combining semantic grounding with fine-grained spatial detail. The second is the dataset: real examples combined with synthetically generated table, chart, and key-value pair data — the structured business information stored in documents, such as invoice numbers, dates, and names. The result is models that outpace other similarly-sized models available today. The team also recently released ChartNet, a million-scale chart dataset, that was used to train the new models.
